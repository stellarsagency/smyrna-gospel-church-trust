About the Trust

A charity built on love, serving those who need us most.

The Smyrna Gospel Church of Pakistan Trust is a registered humanitarian charity founded on 17 May 1992 by Rev. Lal Shahab (Founder & Chairman) and led alongside Joint Secretary Pastor Brandan Julius. For over three decades we have brought food, medical aid, shelter, and education to orphans, displaced families, and vulnerable communities across Pakistan and beyond.

Registered as KAR/EAST/131/2026 under Schedule-I, MoA under the Sindh Trust Rules, 2020, we are committed to full transparency — every donated rupee reaching those who need it most.
