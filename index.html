Our Mission

Humanitarian aid when it matters most.

The Smyrna Gospel Church of Pakistan Trust exists to bring practical relief and lasting hope to communities facing crisis. We respond to urgent needs — food shortages, medical emergencies, loss of shelter, and barriers to education — with dignity, speed, and transparency.

Registered under Schedule-I, MoA under the Sindh Trust Rules, 2020, the Trust is governed by a dedicated board and operates with full legal accountability, audited financial records, and a firm commitment to seeing every donated rupee reach those who need it.

  • ✓Rapid responseEmergency relief deployed within 72 hours of assessment.
  • ✓Full transparencyPublic financials and end-to-end reporting on every project.
  • ✓Local reachCommunity-rooted teams who know each region intimately.
